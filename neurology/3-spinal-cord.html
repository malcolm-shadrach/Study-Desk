Gray Horns (Unmyelinated Cell Bodies)
Anterior / VentralMotor. Cell bodies of the somatic nervous system — controls skeletal muscles. Somatic nerve fibers exit here via the ventral ramus.
Posterior / DorsalSensory. Cell bodies that receive peripheral sensations. Sensory fibers enter here from the dorsal root ganglion.
Central canalLocated between the horns (at the central commissure). Carries cerebrospinal fluid (CSF).
White Columns (Myelinated Tracts)
AnteriorCarries touch and pressure pathways.
LateralCarries pain and temperature pathways.
Dorsal (Posterior)Carries proprioception and fine discriminative touch.
Meninges & Surrounding Spaces (inside → out)
Pia materInnermost layer. Directly adheres to the spinal cord surface.
Subarachnoid spaceSpace between the pia mater and arachnoid mater. Contains CSF.
Arachnoid materThin layer on the inner side of the subdural space, surrounding the subarachnoid space.
Subdural spaceThin potential space between arachnoid mater and dura mater. Contains bridging veins.
Dura mater — meningeal layerInner dural layer; lies just outside the subdural space, closer to the cord.
Epidural spaceBetween the two dural layers. Contains fat and blood vessels.
Dura mater — periosteal layerOutermost layer. Adheres directly to the bone of the vertebral canal.
Nerve Roots & Associated Structures
Dorsal root ganglionContains unipolar (first-order) neurons. Receives sensory input from the periphery and relays it to the posterior gray horn.
Ventral ramusCarries motor fibers from the anterior gray horn out to skeletal muscles.
Dorsal ramusBranch of the spinal nerve that exits posteriorly.
Vertebral Arteries
PathRun upward through the transverse foramen of cervical vertebrae.
DestinationFuse to form the basilar artery, feeding the posterior portion of the Circle of Willis.
