1

Cerebrum — largest part of the brain

Structure

  • Right hemisphere
  • Left hemisphere
  • Corpus callosum (connects both)

Function

Higher thinking, voluntary movement, language, sensation

Four lobes

Frontal

Motor control, decision-making

Parietal

Sensation, spatial awareness

Temporal

Hearing, memory

Occipital

Vision

2

Diencephalon — deep within the brain

Thalamus

Relay station for sensory information — routes signals to the correct cortical area

Hypothalamus

Regulates temperature, hunger, thirst, sleep, and hormonal control

3

Brainstem — vital life functions

Three parts

  • Midbrain
  • Pons
  • Medulla oblongata

Controls

  • Breathing
  • Heart rate
  • Blood pressure
  • Brain ↔ spinal cord connection
4

Cerebellum — posterior (back of brain)

Role

  • Coordination of movement
  • Balance
  • Precision of motor activity

Key point

Does not initiate movement — it fine-tunes movements initiated by the cerebrum

5

Internal structures (model dissection)

Ventricles

Fluid-filled spaces containing CSF (cerebrospinal fluid) — cushions and protects the brain

Corpus callosum

Large white matter tract bridging the two hemispheres — enables left-right communication
